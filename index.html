SLIME SOCCER

Retro Arcade Edition

HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Score more goals than your opponent before time runs out!

Player 1 Controls (Red Slime - Right Side)

← → Move Left/Right
↑ Jump
↓ Grab/Release Ball
← → (while grabbing) Rotate Aim

Player 2 Controls (Cyan Slime - Left Side)

A D Move Left/Right
W Jump
S Grab/Release Ball
A D (while grabbing) Rotate Aim

Anti-Camping Rule

Don't stay in your own goal for too long or you'll receive a penalty!

Gameplay Tips

• Grab the ball to control it and aim your shot
• Use rotation to change your shooting angle
• Time your jumps to intercept high balls
• Stay active - camping is penalized!
